Projects

Church of the Herd of God

SF · 2027

A church for holy rollers, gnostic knowers, and millenarians of the coming dawn. A collaboration between The Classics Department, Fifth Wall Productions, and Emmett Shear.

Psychomagic 9

NYC · 2027

A sly, intoxicating work of gamified theater and alchemical spectacle, taking place on a film set that is either careening toward artistic glory or descending into hell. Written by Morgane Ciot and Miracle Jones. Directed by Evan Neiden.

Sim House

Everywhere · 2027

4 actors enter a house. They are now Sims. They need to be told what to do. The internet is in full control through a Twitch stream, and they have 72 hours to decide what happens. Written and directed by Jack Styles.

Mystical City Universal

SF · 2028

In 1533, a group of Anabaptists took over a small town in Germany and turned it into a free-love doomsday cult. Mystical City Universal is a magical-revisionist adaptation of a real-life apocalypse story, brimming with Lynchian high strangeness and death metal romance. In development.
