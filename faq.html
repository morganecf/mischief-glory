FAQ

What are my donor benefits?
  • $50k and above
    • Tickets: Complimentary tickets to each of our shows
    • Events: Invitations for you and a +1 to funder dinners and exclusive events with our rolodex of magical artistes and performer friends
  • $150k and above
    • Private parties: The opportunity to host private events in our immersive venue after hours. One event for every $150k donated.
    • EP credit: Optional credit as executive producer of the show your donation supported.
  • $500k and above
    • Join the board: Join the board of directors and influence the fate of human taste.
    • Bespoke experience: We’ll collaborate with you to produce a custom dream experience for you and your friends.

Benefits for smaller donors are arranged on a case-by-case basis.

Can I earmark my donation for a specific project?

Loosely, yes. We’re happy to discuss directing your donation toward a particular project, but ultimately, we retain full discretion over how funds are allocated. If a project falls through or doesn’t secure enough funding, we will redirect those funds toward other projects on our slate.

What happens to revenue generated from your shows?

We’re a nonprofit. That means we reinvest any surplus revenue into future productions after covering operating expenses. Revenue in excess of the current project will fund other nonprofit projects produced by Mischief & Glory, Inc.

How do you ensure that projects comply with nonprofit requirements?

For now, all donations are processed through our fiscal sponsor, Counterpart, which oversees the disbursement of funds and provides fiscal oversight to help ensure that the project complies with nonprofit requirements.

Once we transition to our own 501(c)(3), we’ll maintain compliance through standard nonprofit governance practices, including board oversight, financial controls, proper documentation of expenditures, and adherence to IRS rules governing charitable funds and activities.

What is Counterpart?

Counterpart is an established fiscal sponsor that helps artists raise and administer funds for charitable projects. Through its sponsorship, our project can receive tax-deductible donations under Counterpart’s 501c3 status without having to establish a separate nonprofit before fundraising.

The downside is that they charge a 7% fee on any funds we raise. Please keep this in mind when making a donation.

Why are you a nonprofit?

Theater is an expensive, high-risk undertaking. A vast majority of ambitious artistic work depends on nonprofit funding; without it, most art simply wouldn’t exist.

We’re less interested in conventional business than in creating prototypes for the next wave of theater. A nonprofit structure gives us the freedom to take risks, pursue unconventional ideas, and develop work whose value may not be immediately measurable in dollars (much like long-term science funding).
